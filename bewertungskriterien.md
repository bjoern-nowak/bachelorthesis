## Written elaboration:

- Outline of the work
  - The work is well structured. The work follows a common thread. Sequence of chapters/sections follows a recognizable order.
- Introduction: motivation, introduction, delimitation
  - Introduction: The research topic is classified and relevance is justified (if possible supported by sources). The need for the findings of the work is presented.
  - Research questions are present, precisely formulated and pointed. The object of research is clearly definedt.
  - Delimitation: Alternative approaches and methods are named and the chosen approach is justified.
  - Procedure and reading guide: The procedure for dealing with the research question is named and the structure of the work is derived from it.
  - Factual accuracy*, structuring*, scope*
- Presentation of the basics
  - Completeness: All the basics for understanding the work are introduced and appropriate primary sources are referenced.
  - Relevance: Only fundamentals relevant to the present work are introduced.
- Representation of Related Work
  - Completeness: Sufficient and understandable presentation of the relevant related approaches is provided.
  - Relevance: Only approaches relevant to the present work are introduced."
  - Factual accuracy*, structuring*, scope*.
- Presentation of experimental setup, model equations, methodological approach
  - Experimental setup or model equations/implementation
    - are presented in full. Parameters used, assumptions, etc. are substantiated by sources or are justified by explanations/estimates. The presentation corresponds to the implementation in the work.
  - Execution of experiments or simulation:
    - are presented in such a way that the analysis could be repeated by a third party. The presentation corresponds to the implementation in the work.
  - Factual accuracy*, structuring*, scope*
- Presentation of results and discussion
  - Results
    - The analysis is complete; all aspects to be analyzed or accomplished in the time frame have been shown.
  - Discussion:
    - The observed phenomena are plausibilized e.g. by physical considerations or comparisons with sources. Explanatory approaches are provided. Uncertainties in the interpretation are named as these.
  - Factual accuracy*, structuring*, scope*
- Summary, outlook, recommendation for action
  - Summary:
    - Results and findings of the work are correctly summarized. Only the most important aspects are highlighted.
  - Recommendation for action:
    - Recommendations for further action are derived from the results.
  - Weaknesses of the investigations
    - are highlighted and solutions are shown.
  - Outlook:
    - Further work resulting from the findings is logically derived and the added value of this is presented.
  - Factual accuracy*, structuring*, scope*
- Literature work
  - Citation style:
    - A scientific citation style has been applied consistently and correctly so that a distinction can be made between own and adopted thoughts.
  - Quality of the source work:
    - The sources used are up-to-date and scientifically reliable. The sources are correctly evaluated (without falsification). If possible, these are attached to the work.
  - Material correctness of the list:
    - All sources used in the text are complete and correctly cited in the bibliography.
- Scientific writing style
  - Formulations:
    - are reader friendly (clear, easy-to-understand sentence constructions, apt choice of words, consistent use of terms, etc.).
  - Terminology:
    - A scientifically correct technical term is used.
  - Orthography/Grammar:
    - There are no spelling or grammatical errors.
- Formal aspects
  - Layout:
    - The title page, leading text pages, all text pages, and trailing text pages are designed to be legible (size, outlining) in layout (margins, line spacing) and numbered correctly.
  - Figures and tables:
    - are uniform, clear and attractively designed.
  - Front page:
    - is present and correct in form and content.
  - Affidavit:
    - is available and signed in the work.
  - Directories:
    - All required indexes are correctly created and in a sensible order** (table of contents, list of abbreviations, list of symbols, list of figures, list of tables, list of references).
  - Numbering:
    - Figures and tables are provided with numbered superscriptions or captions. Equations are clearly numbered.
  - References:
    - All figures/tables have been referenced in the text.

## Processing phase

- Work organization
  - The task was processed systematically in a meaningful sequence.
  - Intermediate results were presented in a structured form.
  - The calculations/measurements carried out were carefully prepared and correct.
  - The agreed time schedule was adhered to.
- Independence
  - Based on the literature provided, comparable problems/methods were researched independently.
  - The candidate has proposed his/her own solutions and developed solution concepts.
  - The procedure and the results were independently critically scrutinized and checked for plausibility.
  - The results were independently interpreted and conclusions drawn about the problem.
  - The candidate has shown a high level of commitment and hard work.
- Interaction with the supervisor
  - Appointments made were kept on time.
  - Suggestions for changes made by the supervisor were critically examined and implemented.
  - The support offered was used to an appropriate extent (consultation in the case of important questions).

## Quality of the results

- Objectives of the task, technical core
  - The task or proposal was fully implemented and the defined goals were achieved (if external influences made this "impossible", this is not considered negative).
  - The core content of the work (programming, execution of experiments, formalization, etc.) was carried out at a high technical level.
- Quality of the created artifact
  - The artifact fulfills the described functionality.
  - The artifact is cleanly created, e.g., appropriate variable names were chosen, and functionality in functions and classes was outsourced in a target-oriented manner.
  - The functionality of the artifact is sufficiently documented, e.g. in code comments.
- Bonus points
  - The task was very complex and required an above-average effort (only evaluate if applicable).
  - The results of this work can subsequently be published in this form "publication readiness" (only evaluate if applicable).
  - The artifact has been developed so well that this can be directly reused (e.g. tests are included).

## *Factual accuracy, structuring, scope

- Factual accuracy:
  - Only factually correct statements are made.
- Structuring:
  - Thought processes are seamless, consistent, and logically structured. The text has been given a sensible microstructure through paragraphs and page breaks. Statements are well-founded and flanked by appropriate examples.
- Scope:
  - The presentation is appropriate in scope and purposeful. There is no unnecessary rewriting and embellishment.
